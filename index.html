a letter for Bhawana♡

Bhawana, I keep thinking about our first meeting. I know it was short, and perhaps it was uncomfortable or not what we had expected. I don't know exactly how you felt, but I know that I was happy to finally be beside you. You did not have to look perfect or make the day perfect for it to matter to me.

I sometimes compare myself to you and feel like I am not good-looking enough for you. But I know love should not be a competition about who is more beautiful. I loved you for who you are, not because you had to be perfect. I wish I could stop feeling like I was not enough, and I wish we could speak honestly about what changed between us.

I remember the little things I tried to do for you, including buying you things and giving what I could. I did not have much money that day—only around NPR 2,000—and I still wish I could have done more. Please know that what I gave came from a sincere place, not from how much it cost.

I know you have your own feelings and choices. Hearing that you may be with someone else hurts deeply, especially when I am still looking through our old chats, photos, and videos and missing you. I cannot make you choose me, and I do not want to use our memories to make you feel guilty. I only wish we could have one honest conversation about whether there is anything left for us to rebuild.

I have known you through different stages of growing up, and it is hard for me to accept that our lives may now be going in different directions. I still care about you deeply. But I also know that a future together can only happen if we both freely want it and treat each other with honesty and respect.

I love you, Bhawana. Our first meeting may not have been perfect, but it was real—and I am grateful I got to meet you.

Yours, always ♡

OUR FIRST MEETING ♡

For years, we knew each other through messages, calls, and video. Then we finally met in person. I know the day was short, and maybe it felt awkward or different from what either of us had pictured.

But for me, being there with you still mattered. I was happy simply because it was you. I had only about NPR 2,000 with me, and I wish I could have done more or given you more. I couldn't that day, but the care behind what I did was real.

Maybe you felt differently, and I can't decide what that day meant to you. I only want to remember it honestly—not as a perfect movie scene, but as our real first meeting.

After everything,
I still choose you.

For nearly five years, I have carried you in my heart. I watched you grow through so many stages of life, and I imagined being beside you as we both grew older. That history means a lot to me, but I know it does not mean you owe me a future. You have to be free to choose what is right for you. You were the person I wanted to talk to, the person I looked for in my notifications, and the person I imagined sharing my future with. Our first and only meeting, our calls, our videos, our old chats and your photos mean more to me than I know how to explain. I know our meeting was short and may not have felt how we expected, but I was still happy because it was you. I had only around NPR 2,000 that day, and I wish I could have done more for you. I could not give you everything I wanted to, but my feelings were never measured by money or gifts.

Lately, I have been hurting in ways I don't always know how to say. The thought of losing you, and hearing that you may be with someone else while I'm here missing us, has left me feeling lonely. I still find myself looking through our old memories and wishing I could return to the moments when we felt close. I won't pretend that this doesn't hurt, and I won't pretend that I have stopped loving you.

If I made mistakes, I want to understand them and work on them. If we have hurt each other, I wish we could talk honestly, listen without fighting, and see whether there is still a way forward. I can't change the past or ask you to ignore what you feel. I can only tell you that my love was real, my loyalty was real, and the future I pictured with you was real to me.

I still wish we could begin again—not by pretending nothing happened, or ignoring the fact that things may have changed, but by rebuilding trust slowly, with honesty, respect, and effort from both of us. I want a love where we both feel chosen, safe, heard, and happy. I want that with you, but I know it has to be something we both freely want.

Bhawana, I love you more than these words can hold. I miss you. I miss us. And even with a hurting heart, a part of me still hopes our story isn't finished. If your heart has already made its choice, it will hurt, but I won't ask you to stay out of guilt. I just needed you to know what you have meant—and still mean—to me.
